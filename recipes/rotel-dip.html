ROTEL DIP RECIPE

The perfect meaty, cheesy, and spicy dip for fall and football

Rotel Dip is one of the ultimate comfort foods—rich, creamy, cheesy, and loaded with savory flavor. Made with melted cheese, seasoned meat, and Rotel tomatoes with green chilies, it's the kind of appetizer that disappears within minutes at any party or game day gathering.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Spicy sausage
  • 16 oz Velveeta or any melting cheese
  • 2 cans of Rotel
  • 1 8oz block of cream cheese
  • Tortilla Chips

Steps To Make

  1. Brown the sausage
  2. Drain the sausage
  3. Empty the sausage into a slow cooker
  4. Open both cans of rotel, drain one and empty both cans into slow cooker
  5. Cut 16oz of Velveeta into cubes and place in slow cooker
  6. Place cream cheese into slow cooker
  7. Heat until everything is melted
  8. Stir and serve

Buffalo Chicken Dip

The ulimate game day appetizer. Buffalo Chicken Dip is the undisputed champ of game day foods

Buffalo Chicken Dip is the perfect combination of creamy, cheesy, and spicy. Made with tender shredded chicken, rich cream cheese, tangy buffalo sauce, and melted cheese, it's a crowd favorite that delivers bold flavor in every bite and pairs perfectly with tortilla chips, crackers, or fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 1 rotissere chicken
  • 1 packet ranch dressing mix
  • Franks Red Hot Buffalo Wing Sauce
  • 1 8oz block of cream cheese
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Tortilla Chips

Steps To Make

  1. Start by pulling and shredding the chicken
  2. Place the chicken in a skillet on the stove on low/med heat
  3. Add some Franks Red Hot Buffalo Sauce to taste
  4. When chicken is hot, add the 8oz of cream cheese
  5. When cream cheese is melted, mix in some shredded cheddar cheese
  6. Add your ranch mix packet and combine
  7. Remove from heat and place in a over safe dish
  8. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees
  9. While oven heats, add shredded cheese to the top of the dip
  10. When oven is ready, place dip in uncovered
  11. Remove when cheese is golden brown and melted
  12. Let cool and enjoy

Chicken Alfredo with Broccoli

The ultimate Italian Sunday family dinner to please

Chicken Alfredo is a rich and creamy pasta dish made with tender chicken, fettuccine noodles, Parmesan cheese, butter, and garlic. It's a comforting Italian-inspired meal that's perfect for a family dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 12 oz fettuccine pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1½ cups grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley (optional, for garnish)

Steps To Make

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the fettuccine according to the package directions. Drain and set aside.
  2. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the chicken for 6–7 minutes per side until fully cooked. Remove from the pan and slice into strips.
  4. In the same skillet, melt the butter and sauté the minced garlic for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  5. Pour in the heavy cream and bring it to a gentle simmer.
  6. Gradually whisk in the Parmesan cheese until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  7. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss until evenly coated in the Alfredo sauce.
  8. Top with the sliced chicken and garnish with fresh parsley if desired.
  9. Serve immediately while hot.
